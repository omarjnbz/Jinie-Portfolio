House · Afro House · Progressive · Deep House · Melodic

Born in Delhi and schooled by the underground, Jinie Bhatnagar moves between the warm pulse of Afro House and the hypnotic architecture of Progressive — a sound that feels as at home in a rooftop sunrise set as it does in a dark basement.

Her selections pull from the deeper wells: Stephan Bodzin's cosmic tension, Hernan Cattaneo's long-form storytelling, the melodic edge of Guy J. Every mix is a slow reveal — patience over punch, texture over volume.

Delhi to the world. The intention is always the same — create a space where the music holds the room and every track feels like it was always meant to follow the one before it.
